High-Throughput eBPF Telemetry Benchmarks in Distributed Microservice Architectures

Author: Aman Kumar (@aman179102)
ORCID: 0009-0002-5219-5949
Scholar: cVhFkv4AAAAJ
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24493466
Download Full Paper (PDF) Benchmark Datasets
Abstract: Capturing low-latency distributed traces in cloud microservices traditionally introduces unacceptable runtime overhead through user-space interceptors and sidecar proxies. In this paper, we evaluate kernel-space extended Berkeley Packet Filters (eBPF) utilizing memory-mapped circular ring buffers (BPF_MAP_TYPE_RINGBUF) to collect L4/L7 socket metrics non-invasively. Empirical evaluations demonstrate sub-0.5% CPU degradation under 500,000 requests per second.
