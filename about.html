I like problems where the interface is simple and the engineering is not.

My work sits across the stack: React and Next.js on the product side, Python and FastAPI in services, Go when the problem is closer to the metal, and PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker, and Kubernetes underneath. Principles

How I try
to work.

01

Ship the smallest honest version.

A working slice teaches more than a polished plan. Start narrow, make it real, then earn the next layer.

02

Make failure visible.

Logs, metrics, and clear errors are not polish. They are how software stays trustworthy when conditions change.

03

Leave the next person a map.

Good names, small surfaces, and honest documentation are part of the product, not an afterthought.
